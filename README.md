## Happy 35th Anniversary, Linux!
In celebrations of Linux's 35th anniversary, releasing a collection of 10,000 unique images of Tux generated by combining hand-drawn layers. 
Don't reuse that same Tux PNG. Here are 10,000 clean ones, free, for your presentations, event, docs, meetup slides, stickers, etc.
Variations include different backgrounds, body lines, name tag, headwear, eyes, eyewear, nose shape, hand posture, foot posture, accessories, sports equipment, laptops, celebrations, etc.

Have fun with it!
